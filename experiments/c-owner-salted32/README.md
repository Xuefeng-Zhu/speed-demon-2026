# C salted owner with 32-bit fingerprint — unselected

Baseline is the unselected 16-byte salted owner candidate SHA-256 `0aa4cbccf08b05a59aadbe7eaf23a931e468a95f76463a95100a142e8221bc08`, not the selected solution. Candidate `073fe4f9caf0cc33f3650110c52df28cb1a5a13f6ed4b24c9cb514d45dd07e13` keeps the full 64-bit Fx hash for owner routing but stores its low 32 bits in a 12-byte event. Each owner uses a randomly seeded u32 fingerprint map; candidate names are still compared byte-for-byte, and after eight unequal names share a fingerprint the bucket promotes to a separately seeded exact-name map. Fingerprint collisions cannot change counts; they can alter runtime. No data, answers or credentials are stored.

Broad local checks passed 120 mixed reference cases, 357 CRLF boundaries, 20 long-name/sentinel and forced-fallback cases, 20 groups through all 17,576 uppercase codes, 12 owner/full-hash collision and growth cases, 1,007,168 owner-range checks and the exact-source 50,000 same-fingerprint promotion suite. A real >4 GiB mapping was not run, and this remains an unselected prototype pending actual x86 checks. The parser retains official §2.2/2.3 generated-data assumptions.

Two 15-pair shared ARM native full-data batches plus warmups matched all 64 hashes. Candidate/baseline median wall was 0.13396/0.15749 s in a heavily contended batch, then 0.10148/0.10401 s; CPU 0.52752/0.56433 and 0.53527/0.53883 s. Maximum native RSS was ~268–270 MB versus ~285–287 MB. Data and raw timing samples are in the JSON reports. The second result establishes at most a small local difference, not an x86 or official speed win. No Mac Docker was used.

Independent review found no correctness defect under §2.2–2.3. The reproducible `independent-review.py` checks 50,000 synthetic distinct full hashes sharing one low32 fingerprint/owner, with exact promotion and 100,000 name/group checks. It also searches up to 1,000,000 syntactically valid names and found two distinct real Fx64 hashes with matching low32 and owner after 312,631 names; both remained separate through group boundaries. These fixtures do not establish occurrence in generator seeds or target speed. A real >4 GiB map and target cgroup remain untested.

A separate `independent-end-to-end.py` test uses two syntactically valid names found by the above search (not known generated-seed inputs), 12,000 repeated records with BOMs, quoted LFs and an unmatched group. It matched the official reference at workers 1/2/7/12/32.
